# AgileMD

> AgileMD is a clinical decision support company that embeds real-time predictive analytics and evidence-based clinical pathways directly into hospital electronic health record (EHR) workflows such as Epic and Cerner. Its FDA-cleared eCART Clinical Deterioration Suite scores patient risk from nearly 100 real-time variables, while its Clinical Pathways product standardizes evidence-based order sets and protocols. AgileMD serves 400+ hospitals and 150,000+ clinical users, and is backed by Y Combinator, MATTER, and Rock Health.

Note: AgileMD delivers its software as an EHR-embedded medical device (Epic, Cerner). It does not publish a public developer API, OpenAPI, or developer portal; integration happens through health-system EHR partnerships. ## Products
- [eCART Clinical Deterioration Suite](https://www.agilemd.com/): FDA-cleared AI early-warning system that scores patient deterioration risk in real time inside the EHR.
- [Clinical Pathways](https://www.agilemd.com/): Evidence-based order sets, documentation, and protocols standardized inside the EHR workflow.
